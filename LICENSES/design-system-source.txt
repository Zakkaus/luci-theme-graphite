Vendored stylesheets
====================

Three files under htdocs/luci-static/graphite/ are verbatim copies and are
never edited in place:

  tokens.css      <- ~/design-system/app/tokens.css
  components.css  <- ~/design-system/app/components.css
  shell.css       <- ~/design-system/app/shell.css

They carry no source note of their own, because a note is an edit and these
files have to stay byte-identical to their source for the copy to be checkable
at all:

  diff -q htdocs/luci-static/graphite/tokens.css ~/design-system/app/tokens.css

Anything this project needs on top of them goes in graphite.css, and anything
that turns out to belong upstream is changed in the design system and comes
back through a re-copy. A change made in the copy is the drift the whole
arrangement exists to prevent — it has happened once already, when the design
system gained color-scheme, two-engine scrollbars and a shared brand height
that this package was still patching in locally.

Last synchronised: 2026-08-31.

Lucide icons (menu-graphite.js, header.ut) are a separate matter and are
covered by LICENSES/lucide-ISC.txt.
